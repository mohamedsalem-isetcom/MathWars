MATHWARS

Shoot with equations. Hit every target.

How to play

Type a function of x, like 2x + 5 or sin(x) * 20. Your shot travels along the curve from left to right and stops at the first target it touches.

Blue targets give 10 points, small yellow ones 25, the star 50. Classic gives you 5 shots, 30 seconds each.
